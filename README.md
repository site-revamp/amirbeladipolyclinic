# Amir Beladi Polyclinic Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Amir Beladi Polyclinic is a multi-specialty healthcare and aesthetic clinic located on Al Wasl Road, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The clinic provides dental, dermatology, aesthetic, hair restoration, wellness and laboratory services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic as an integrated dental and aesthetic healthcare provider. It creates a clearer experience for patients exploring treatments, learning about medical professionals and requesting consultations.

The design follows a **Clinical Elegance** direction, combining a refined navy and ivory colour palette with elegant typography and a professional healthcare presentation.

## 2. Main Improvements

Three key improvements were implemented to address the main issues identified during the website review.

### 1. Improved Service Discovery and Navigation

- Introduced a clearer service discovery hierarchy covering Dental, Skin & Aesthetics, Hair and Wellness.
- Organised the main treatment pathways to make the clinic's multi-specialty offering easier to understand.
- Improved navigation so visitors can identify relevant treatment categories more easily.

**Problem addressed:** The previous service presentation made it difficult for visitors to distinguish between the clinic's different specialties.

### 2. Clearer Patient Consultation Journey

- Introduced consultation-first messaging to guide visitors towards appropriate treatment enquiries.
- Added a four-step consultation journey to explain the process of getting started.
- Strengthened appointment and contact calls to action throughout the homepage.

**Problem addressed:** Treatment information needed stronger patient guidance to help visitors understand the next steps before choosing a service.

### 3. Stronger Clinical Credibility and Trust Presentation

- Integrated the clinic's 12+ years of medical experience into the homepage.
- Improved the visibility of medical professionals and their clinical roles.
- Connected treatment information with relevant clinic details, diagnostic capabilities and patient contact options.
- Incorporated the clinic's location, operating hours and contact information.

**Problem addressed:** Important credibility information was not sufficiently connected to service discovery and appointment decisions.

## 3. Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several visual and usability improvements:

- **Colour Palette:** Applied a consistent navy and ivory colour scheme for a refined clinical appearance.
- **Typography:** Combined Cormorant Garamond headings with Manrope body text to create a clear visual hierarchy.
- **Layout:** Introduced a more structured homepage layout with distinct content sections and specialty pathways.
- **Navigation:** Improved navigation structure and treatment categorisation.
- **Visual Elements:** Added six custom SVG icons to support service identification and visual consistency.
- **Mobile Experience:** Implemented responsive layouts, mobile navigation and two persistent mobile call-to-action buttons.
- **Conversion Experience:** Improved the placement and visibility of consultation and contact actions.
- **Content Consistency:** Established a more consistent presentation of services, clinicians and clinic information.

## 4. Technical Implementation

The redesigned homepage includes:

- Standalone HTML implementation.
- Custom responsive CSS styling.
- JavaScript functionality for interactive elements.
- Six custom SVG service icons.
- Responsive navigation and mobile layouts.
- Structured data using JSON-LD.
- Persistent mobile appointment and contact actions.

## 5. Overall Result

The redesigned homepage provides a clearer presentation of Amir Beladi Polyclinic's multi-specialty healthcare services. It improves treatment discovery, introduces a more understandable consultation journey and gives greater prominence to clinical expertise and clinic information.

The new design establishes a consistent visual identity while making appointment enquiries and patient contact more accessible across desktop and mobile devices.
