Helpful information

Before you get in touch.

Some useful starting points for visitors exploring the clinic's services and planning an appointment.

Which service should I ask about?

You can contact the clinic with your main concern rather than choosing a treatment name first. The team can help direct your enquiry to dental care, skin and aesthetics, hair services or wellness and diagnostics.

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Suitability depends on the service and your individual circumstances. Clinical and aesthetic options should be discussed with an appropriate clinician before a treatment decision is made.

Where is Amir Beladi Polyclinic located?

The clinic is located at 351 Al Wasl Road, Al Bada'a, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

What are the clinic's opening hours?

The clinic's published contact information lists its working hours as every day from 10:30 AM to 8:00 PM. Contact the clinic directly to confirm appointment availability.

How can I arrange an appointment?

You can call or WhatsApp the clinic on +971 54 888 1488, or use the official contact page to make an appointment enquiry.
